## How AI actually picks its sources

AI models don't care about your keyword density. They care about semantic clarity, hard data, and authority. Here is what they actually look for:

### 1. Give the answer immediately
Don't bury the lead. If someone asks a question, put the exact answer in the very first sentence. AI systems extract *passages*, not pages. Make your claims self-contained.

### 2. Use hard numbers
AI models heavily favor statistics over fluff. Saying "We are the fastest web agency" is useless. Saying "We deploy websites in 24 hours" gets cited.

### 3. Build third-party authority
AI systems don't just read your site. They cross-reference it. Brands are 6.5x more likely to be cited if they are mentioned on Wikipedia, Reddit, G2, or industry publications. Your reputation off-site matters just as much as your on-site copy.

## The GEO Checklist

If you want Claude or Perplexity to recommend your business, your pages need to look like this:

- [ ] **Direct answers.** First paragraph, no exceptions.
- [ ] **Data.** Statistics backed by cited sources.
- [ ] **Comparison tables.** Use tables for "X vs Y" queries. AI loves extracting tables.
- [ ] **Numbered lists.** Use them for processes and "How to" queries.
- [ ] **Schema markup.** Use JSON-LD to explicitly tell the AI what your page is about (Organization, Service, FAQ).

## The files you are missing

Writing good copy isn't enough anymore. You need to provide machine-readable files for the AI agents that are crawling your site:

### `/llms.txt`
This is essentially `robots.txt` but for AI understanding. It's a plain text file that gives language models a direct, unstyled overview of your product and pricing. 

### `/pricing.md`
If your pricing is locked behind a JavaScript calculator or a "Contact Sales" button, AI agents will skip you and recommend the competitor whose pricing they can actually read. Put your prices in a markdown file.

## Real Questions

### Does GEO replace traditional SEO?
No. Google's AI Overviews still heavily rely on traditional ranking signals. GEO sits on top of your SEO strategy. You still need a fast site, good backlinks, and strong technical foundations.

### How do I track this?
It's manual right now. You need to run your core search queries through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google (with AI Overviews on) and see if your brand appears. There are tools emerging to automate this, but testing it yourself is the best baseline.

### Is this just for big tech companies?
No. Local businesses need GEO desperately. When someone asks an AI "Who is the best plumber in Austin?", you want the AI to cite your business. Having structured, citable content is how you win that recommendation.
