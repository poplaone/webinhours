You don't need a custom design to validate a business.

**The data supports this:**

## When templates win

### 1. You need to prove your idea
If your business needs an online presence today, templates are the only option. We offer over 500 professional templates at WebInHour that can be customized and live by tomorrow. Don't spend months building something nobody wants.

### 2. You want to preserve capital
A startup blowing its budget on a custom website before finding product-market fit is a tragedy. Keep your cash. Use a template. Spend the money on ads or sales.

### 3. Your business model is standard
If you run a local service, a consulting firm, a restaurant, or a standard B2B company, your website needs are not unique. You need a hero section, social proof, pricing, and a contact form. Templates handle this perfectly.

## When you actually need custom design

We aren't saying custom design is dead. It's just overused. You only need a custom build if:

### 1. You are building an actual application
If your site requires complex user dashboards, custom databases, or intricate payment flows, a template won't cut it. You need software engineering, not web design.

### 2. You are an established enterprise
If you are doing $50M a year and need a pixel-perfect design system that perfectly expresses your unique brand identity across 500 pages, pay an agency.

## The 3-second decision matrix

If you are stuck, ask yourself these questions:

1. **Am I validating a new idea?** → Use a template.
2. **Do I have less than $5,000?** → Use a template.
3. **Do I need to launch this month?** → Use a template.
4. **Do I need users to log in and manage complex data?** → Go custom.

**Start with a template.** Invest in professional customization. Upgrade to a fully custom architecture only when your revenue forces you to.

## Real Questions

### Will my site look exactly like my competitors?
No. Once you apply professional brand colors, specific typography, custom imagery, and good copy, the underlying layout disappears. Nobody looks at a website and says, "Ah, they used a split-hero Flexbox layout." They just see your brand.

### Do templates hurt SEO?
No. Google doesn't care if you used a template. Google cares if your site loads fast, works on mobile, and actually answers the user's query. A well-built template site will consistently outrank a slow, poorly coded custom site.
